# March 2026 Variance Analysis — Working Notes

**Author:** FP&A team
**Status:** Internal working draft — not for distribution
**Period:** March 2026 (Month 3 of Q1)

These are my raw notes after the close. Numbers tie to the GL as of the April 2 export. Headcount data from BambooHR pull on April 1.

## Headline

Total OpEx came in at $2,203K against a budget of $2,137K — an unfavorable variance of $66K, or 3.1%. The aggregate number understates the run-rate problem: a meaningful chunk of the offsetting favorable variance is timing-driven and will reverse over Q2 and Q3. True run-rate is closer to $125K unfavorable per month once the timing items normalize.

## Material variances (>$10K or >20%)

**Engineering Salaries & Benefits — favorable $60K (-6.8%).** The single biggest variance in the file. Three open reqs (one Senior Backend, one Staff PM, one Engineering Manager) remain unfilled at month-end. Recruiting confirms two are in late-stage; one is being re-scoped. The salary underrun is purely timing — these reqs are budgeted for FY26 and we expect the savings to reverse in Q2 and Q3 as offers go out. We should not treat this as run-rate savings.

**Marketing Programs — unfavorable $60K (+50%).** Programs spend was $180K against a $120K budget. Driver is the Q1 pipeline acceleration push approved by the CMO and CRO in mid-February — additional paid media on LinkedIn ($35K) and a sponsored webinar series ($20K), with the balance in conference contract amendments. This was an in-quarter decision and does not appear to have been reflected in the latest forecast. The CMO's view is that pipeline created from the push will support Q2/Q3 bookings, but we have not seen the attribution data yet.

**Sales Travel & Entertainment — unfavorable $15K (+50%).** $45K actual vs $30K budget. Two drivers: (1) an unbudgeted three-rep trip to a strategic prospect in EMEA, and (2) Q1 QBR was held in person in Austin rather than virtually, which was the original plan. Sales leadership signed off on both. Likely to repeat in Q2 if EMEA momentum continues — flagging for forecast update.

**G&A Professional Services — unfavorable $35K (+70%).** $85K actual vs $50K budget. Driver is roughly $25K in additional audit fees (auditors expanded scope around revenue recognition for the new product line) and $10K in legal fees for the rewrite of our standard MSA. The audit overage is non-recurring. The legal work is also non-recurring but a similar exercise is queued for Q3 around DPA templates.

**Engineering Software & SaaS — unfavorable $15K (+25%).** Cloud spend (AWS) ran $12K above plan. Engineering attributes this to the new ML inference workloads moving to production earlier than planned. Not a one-time event — needs to be reflected in the forward forecast. Suggest meeting with VP Eng to true up the FY26 cloud line.

## Smaller variances worth noting

- **Marketing T&E favorable $4K (-33%).** Two team members did not attend a planned conference. Minor.
- **Customer Success T&E unfavorable $1K (+17%).** On-site visits to two enterprise renewals. Within tolerance.
- **G&A T&E unfavorable $1K (+20%).** Recruiting travel for the open Engineering reqs. Will normalize as reqs close.

## Headcount

Total budgeted headcount of 133, actual of 128, with 4 open reqs. The 5-person gap is concentrated in Engineering (3 open) and a 1-person gap in Sales (offer accepted, start date April 13). Budgeted comp expense will catch up over Q2 as reqs close.

## Themes and hypotheses

1. **Timing vs run-rate.** Several of the largest variances (Eng salaries, Marketing programs, audit fees) are timing-driven and will reverse or normalize. The aggregate $66K unfavorable position understates the underlying run-rate problem — once the $60K Eng salary timing favorability burns off in Q2/Q3, run-rate is closer to $125K unfavorable per month. Want to flag this prominently when we present.

2. **In-quarter spend decisions are not flowing into the rolling forecast.** The Marketing Programs overage is the clearest case but not the only one. Recommend tightening the in-quarter approval process so anything over $25K hits the forecast within two weeks of approval.

3. **Cloud spend is a forward risk.** Engineering Software is over by 25% this month and the underlying driver (ML workloads in prod) is permanent. We should re-baseline the cloud line for the rest of FY26 and stress test against the bookings forecast.

- Decide whether to roll the Eng salary timing favorability forward in the forecast or hold it as upside.
